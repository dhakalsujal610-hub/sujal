Meet Simona

Simona Badger, LCSW

Simona Badger is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW) with over 15 years of experience in mental health, immigration forensic evaluations, and professional training. Her work is deeply rooted in social justice and a commitment to honoring the resilience of immigrant communities.

Having witnessed the profound intersections of immigration law and mental health, Simona became one of the leading voices in the field of immigration mental health evaluations — training clinicians nationwide and providing expert evaluations that have helped hundreds of individuals successfully navigate the immigration system.

Simona holds a Master of Social Work (MSW) from a nationally accredited program and has maintained licensure while building SB Empowerment into a comprehensive organization serving individuals, families, and professionals.

Forensic Mental Health

Immigration Mental Health Evaluations

An immigration mental health evaluation is a clinical assessment conducted by a licensed mental health professional to document the psychological impact of persecution, trauma, abuse, or hardship in support of immigration cases.

Simona Badger's evaluations are comprehensive, trauma-informed, and written to the highest forensic standards — providing attorneys and immigration courts with the expert documentation needed to support your client's case.

Cases We Support

Asylum ApplicationsPast persecution & fear of return evaluations
VAWA PetitionsDocumenting domestic violence & emotional harm
U-Visa CasesMental health impact of crime victimization

Curriculum

Training Modules

20+ hours of expert instruction across 8 core modules, combining legal frameworks, clinical skills, and hands-on evaluation practice.

  • 01

    Immigration Law Fundamentals

    Overview of visa categories, relief options, and how mental health evaluations fit into legal proceedings.

  • 02

    Psychological Impact of Immigration Trauma

    Understanding PTSD, depression, anxiety, and other conditions common in immigrant populations.

  • 03

    Trauma-Informed Clinical Interviewing

    Ethical interview techniques for gathering sensitive history with cultural humility and trauma sensitivity.

  • 04

    Assessment Tools & Standardized Measures

    Selection and administration of culturally validated psychological assessment instruments.

  • 05

    Writing Forensic Evaluation Reports

    Structure, language, and standards for writing court-ready, attorney-usable evaluation reports.

  • 06

    Case Types Deep Dive

    Specific guidance for asylum, VAWA, U-Visa, hardship waivers, and special juvenile immigrant cases.

  • 07

    Ethics, Boundaries & Self-Care

    Professional ethics in forensic work, managing vicarious trauma, and sustainable practice strategies.

  • 08

    Supervised Evaluation Practice

    Guided case examples with feedback, peer review, and supervised report writing practice.
